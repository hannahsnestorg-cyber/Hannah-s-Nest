Joining the Nest

Membership terms.

The few promises that keep the Nest safe for every Sister.

Who can join

Women aged 18 and over who are trying to conceive, have waited, or are carrying loss. A host reviews every request.

What the Nest is

Peer support, faith encouragement and general information. It is not medical care, counselling or therapy, and no one can promise a pregnancy.

What’s shared here stays here

It’s against our rules to tell or forward conversations from the group. We ask every Sister to honour this. That’s why this is a private group.

No selling

No products, herbs, “cures” or promotions. Breaking this means removal straight away.

What you share

You choose what to share. Other members can see your WhatsApp name, number, profile photo and posts. We ask everyone to keep them private, but we can’t control what other people do.

Your story

We never share your story publicly without your written consent.

Removal

Other rule breaks get one gentle reminder, then removal. We may remove anyone who puts Sisters at risk.

Leaving

You can leave any time by exiting the groups or messaging a host. Ask us and we’ll delete your details, as our privacy notice explains.

Changes

We’ll tell Sisters in the Announcements group before these terms change.
