WHO THE NEST IS FOR

A place for every kind of waiting.

Hannah’s Nest is for women in Nigeria and the diaspora who are trying to conceive, living with infertility, or carrying loss. You don’t need a diagnosis to belong here.

Trying to conceive (TTC)

You’ve started trying for a baby and want company on the journey, however long it has been.

Primary infertility

You’ve been trying for 12 months or more and haven’t yet had a pregnancy.

Secondary infertility

You’ve been pregnant before, with or without a child today, and it’s now hard to conceive again.

Pregnancy loss

Miscarriage, ectopic or molar pregnancy, stillbirth, or recurrent pregnancy loss (two or more losses).

Unexplained infertility

Your tests say “normal”, but the baby hasn’t come and no one can tell you why.

Fertility treatment

Ovulation induction, IUI, IVF or ICSI, donor eggs or sperm, or surrogacy. Every round, every wait.

Male factor infertility

Your husband’s results are part of the story. You’re carrying it too, and you belong here.

After the wait

Your baby has come after a long wait, and you want to hold the hands of Sisters still waiting.

Not sure where you fit? You still belong. Every Sister is welcome, whatever her story.

YOUR JOIN AGREEMENT

Joining the Nest means making a few promises to every Sister. On the joining form you’ll tick each one and sign with your name or nickname:

  • I’m 18 or over.
  • I’ll keep what’s shared in the Nest private: no forwarding or retelling.
  • I’ll follow the house rules, including no selling, and I understand breaking them can mean removal.
  • I understand the Nest is peer support, not medical advice, and I’ll speak to my own doctor about my health.
  • I understand other Sisters in the group can see my WhatsApp name, number and profile photo, and I’ll set my privacy the way I’m comfortable with.
  • I’ve read and agree to the membership terms and the privacy notice.
Read the membership terms
  • Who can join. Women aged 18 and over who are trying to conceive, have waited, or are carrying loss. A host reviews every request.
  • What the Nest is. Peer support, faith encouragement and general information. It is not medical care, counselling or therapy, and no one can promise a pregnancy.
  • What’s shared here stays here. It’s against our rules to tell or forward conversations from the group. We implore every Sister to abide by this. That’s why this is a private group.
  • No selling. No products, herbs, “cures” or promotions. Breaking this means removal straight away.
  • What you share. You choose what to share. Other members can see your WhatsApp name, number and posts; we ask everyone to keep them private, but we can’t control what other people do.
  • Your story. We never share your story publicly without your written consent.
  • Removal. Other rule breaks get one gentle reminder, then removal. We may remove anyone who puts Sisters at risk.
  • Leaving. You can leave any time by exiting the groups or messaging a host.
  • Changes. We’ll tell Sisters in the Announcements group before these terms change.

The story in our logo Read ›

Look closely, Sister. There’s a little bit of you in it.

The doorway is God’s house and ours, always open. Like the house at Shiloh, where Hannah poured out her heart and was heard.

The nest is you, held. “Even the sparrow has found a home, and the swallow a nest for herself.” (Psalm 84:3)

The bud is you in your season. Not open yet, but alive, growing, and already beautiful.

The two side petals are your Sisters, one on each side, holding you up the way Aaron and Hur held up Moses’ hands (Exodus 17:12).

The warm clay colour is a gentle reminder: “we are the clay, and You are the potter” (Isaiah 64:8). You are still being shaped, by loving hands.

Hannah’s Nest is peer support and education, not medical care. Always speak with your doctor about diagnosis and treatment.
