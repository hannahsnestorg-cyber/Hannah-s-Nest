Privacy notice

Your privacy, in plain words.

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

The short version. We collect only what we need to welcome you and keep the Nest safe. Your journal stays on your phone, and if you back it up, it is locked so even we can’t read it. We never sell your details.

Who we are

Hannah’s Nest is a peer support community for women trying to conceive, run by Hannah’s Nest (registered business name, CAC No. 10002512), Lagos, Nigeria. Email us any time at info@hannahsnest.org.

What we collect, and when

  • When you ask to join: the name or nickname you give, your WhatsApp number, where you live, how long you have been waiting, and your signed agreement. You never need to share medical details.
  • When you send a question, guide request, prayer request or a Petal: what you type, and a nickname if you add one.
  • When you message us on WhatsApp or by email: your message and contact details.

What stays on your phone

Your daily check-ins, journal and keepsake cards are saved in your own browser on your device. We can’t see them. Clearing your browser, or tapping “Clear my journal from this phone” on the My journal page, deletes them.

If you share a phone or browser, anyone using it could open your journal. Clear it whenever you need to.

If you choose to back up your journal

“Save my journal” is optional. If you use it, you create an account with an email and password. Your journal is locked (encrypted) on your phone with your password before it leaves your device, so we only ever hold your email and scrambled text we cannot read. If you forget your password, we can’t recover your journal. To delete your backup, email us.

Visit counting

To see how many Sisters the site is reaching, the site counts visits and taps on The Well. It stores a random code in your browser so a visit isn’t counted twice in a day. We don’t collect your name, number, location or IP address for this, and we only ever see totals. Clearing your browser removes the code.

Why we use your information

To review your request, welcome you into the WhatsApp circle, reply to your questions and prayer requests, contact you about the Nest, and keep the community safe. We do this with your consent, and you can withdraw it any time.

Services we use

We use trusted services to run the Nest. They process information for us under their own terms and safeguards, and some of them store data outside Nigeria (for example in the United States or Europe).

  • Tally: our joining, question and Petal forms.
  • Google Sheets and Gmail: our copy of form replies, and our email.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): the private circle and messages with hosts.
  • Netlify: hosts this website.
  • Supabase: the optional encrypted journal backup and the visit totals.
  • Google Fonts: the lettering on this site. Your browser connects to Google to load it.
  • Behold: shows our latest Instagram posts on the home page.

Who can see it

Our hosts, who welcome you and look after the circle. We may share information if the law requires it. We don’t sell your details, ever.

Inside the WhatsApp circle

Other members of the groups you join may see your WhatsApp name, number, profile photo and anything you post. We ask every Sister to keep what is shared private and we remove anyone who breaks the house rules, but we can’t control what other people do. Share only what you are comfortable sharing, and set your WhatsApp privacy the way you like.

How long we keep it

  • Members: while you are a member, and up to 12 months after you leave.
  • Requests we can’t accept, or that you withdraw: deleted within 30 days.
  • Questions, prayer requests and Petals: up to 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

We keep information longer only if the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, withdraw your consent, or object to how we use it. Email info@hannahsnest.org and we’ll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or app can promise to be completely secure.

Age

The Nest is for women aged 18 and over.

Not medical advice

Hannah’s Nest offers peer support and general information. Nothing shared by us, our guests or other members is medical advice. Always speak to your own doctor about your health and treatment.

Changes

If this notice changes, we’ll update the date below and tell Sisters in the Announcements group.
