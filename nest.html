WHO THE NEST IS FOR

A place for every kind of waiting.

Hannah’s Nest is for women in Nigeria and the diaspora who are trying to conceive, living with infertility, or carrying loss.

Trying to conceive (TTC)

You’ve started trying for a baby and want company on the journey, however long it has been.

Primary infertility

You’ve been trying for 12 months or more and haven’t yet had a pregnancy.

Secondary infertility

You’ve been pregnant before, with or without a child today, and it’s now hard to conceive again.

Pregnancy loss

You’ve lost a pregnancy and are carrying that grief, gently, while you hope again.

THE PRIVATE WHATSAPP CIRCLE

A closed group. By invitation only.

Hannah’s Nest lives on WhatsApp, the app you already open every day. It's a closed WhatsApp Community: nobody can join from a link they found online. Every Sister is welcomed in personally by a host.

Screened and closed

Women only, every request checked by a host. Sellers and strangers never get in.

Calm, not chaotic

An announcements channel, The Parlour for daily chat, and small rooms by topic. Quiet hours every night.

Questions Sisters ask

Who is the Nest for?

Any Nigerian woman trying to conceive, first child or second, in Nigeria or abroad.

Do I have to use my real name?

No. Nicknames are welcome, and you can join with a second number if that feels safer.

Is it religious?

We are a Christian, faith-friendly home, and prayer sits next to real medical information. Nobody is pushed either way.

Will doctors give me medical advice?

Our experts share general information and answer questions in sessions. For your own treatment, always speak with your doctor. We can point you to trusted clinics.

YOUR PRIVACY

We only ask for what we need to welcome you, and we don’t sell it.

Read our privacy notice

Who we are

Hannah’s Nest is a peer support community run by Hannah’s Nest (registered business name, CAC No. 10002512), Lagos, Nigeria. You can reach us at info@hannahsnest.org.

What we collect

When you ask to join, we collect what you type into the form: a name or nickname, your WhatsApp number, where you live and how long you have been waiting. You don’t need to share any medical details to join.

What stays on your phone

Your daily check-in, journal and keepsake cards are saved only in your own browser on your device. We can’t see them. Clearing your browser deletes them.

Why we use it

To review your request, add you to the WhatsApp circle, contact you about the Nest, and keep the community safe. We only do this with your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Who can see it

Our hosts, who welcome you into the circle. Services we use to run the Nest, such as WhatsApp and our website and email tools, process data under their own terms. We may share information if the law requires it. We don’t sell your details.

Inside the WhatsApp circle

Other members of the groups you join may see your WhatsApp name, number and anything you post. We ask every Sister to keep what is shared private and we remove anyone who breaks the house rules, but we can’t control what other people do. Share only what you are comfortable sharing.

How long we keep it

While you are a member, and for up to 12 months after you leave, unless the law asks us to keep it longer. Then we delete it.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or app can promise to be completely secure.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, withdraw your consent, or object to how we use it. Email us at info@hannahsnest.org. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Not medical advice

Hannah’s Nest offers peer support and general information. Nothing shared by us, our guests or other members is medical advice. Always speak to your own doctor about your health and treatment.

Age

The Nest is for women aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update this page and the date below.

The story in our logo Read ›

Look closely, Sister. There’s a little bit of you in it.

The doorway is God’s house and ours, always open. Like the house at Shiloh, where Hannah poured out her heart and was heard.

The nest is you, held. “Even the sparrow has found a home, and the swallow a nest for herself.” (Psalm 84:3)

The bud is you in your season. Not open yet, but alive, growing, and already beautiful.

The two side petals are your Sisters, one on each side, holding you up the way Aaron and Hur held up Moses’ hands (Exodus 17:12).

The warm clay colour is a gentle reminder: “we are the clay, and You are the potter” (Isaiah 64:8). You are still being shaped, by loving hands.

Hannah’s Nest is peer support and education, not medical care. Always speak with your doctor about diagnosis and treatment.
